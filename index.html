About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Oklahoma, specializing in power systems and energy technologies. My research focuses on the aging of electrical machines operating under dynamic operating conditions imposed by grid flexibility provision and how they impact the lifetime of electric motors. I am interested in exploring control and data-driven techniques to solve challenges related to grid modernization.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I served for several years as an Electrical Engineer with Renewable Energy for Rural Livelihood (Nepal Government / UNDP Nepal), spearheading solar mini-grid projects, emergency technical rehabilitation, and formulating national standard feasibility guidelines.

Projects

💻 LabVIEW‑based Power Measurement System

Developed a custom LabVIEW application to perform real-time acquisition, processing, and data logging of real, reactive, and apparent power for a Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) fed induction machine. Ensured accuracy and reliability by implementing measurement methodologies in strict compliance with relevant IEEE standards, creating a validated tool for experimental thermal and electrical analysis.

Data Acquisition (DAQ) IEEE Standards LabVIEW Programming Power Measurement VFDs

🌡️ Temperature Control and Automation

Designed a closed-loop automation system using a PLC and Ladder Logic for a temperature-controlled environment. Used cost-effective sensors and implemented safety features, real-time monitoring, and data logging using Click PLC and LED-based heater/fan indicators. Integrated a cost-effective hardware simulation by modeling an expensive thermal sensor with a potentiometer.

Control Systems Data Logging Lab Simulation Ladder Logic PLC (Click)

🤖 Data Driven Temperature Estimation Techniques Using Supervised Machine Learning

Investigated the feasibility of linear regression models for real-time temperature estimation in a Permanent Magnet Synchronous Machine (PMSM) to prevent overheating and failure. Implemented and evaluated regression models (Ordinary Least Squares, Ridge, and Lasso) in Python (Scikit-learn library) by standardizing and preprocessing a large-scale dataset (300 MB) of motor operational parameters. Demonstrated that linear models were insufficient (achieving a max R² value of 0.29 for the multivariable model), proving that motor thermal dynamics are dominated by complex non-linear relationships and motivating the need for autoregressive or recurrent structures.

Data Preprocessing Linear Regression Python Scikit-learn Supervised Machine Learning

🔋 Optimal Placement and Sizing of Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)

Implemented a two-step approach using the Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) algorithm in Python (pandapower library) for the optimal placement and sizing of a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) in an electrical network. Demonstrated that optimum BESS placement on IEEE 14-bus and 30-bus test systems significantly reduced power losses by 7.5%, confirming that siting BESS at high-load buses maximizes grid efficiency through localized power support.

Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) Power Flow Analysis Python pandapower

📊 Analyzing and Forecasting Electricity Demand

Analyzed ERCOT electricity demand data (2016–2021) by conducting time series decomposition to identify underlying trends, seasonality, and correlations with external factors (temperature, GDP). Developed a monthly forecasting model (seasonal ARIMA(0,1,2)(0,1,1)[12]) achieving a high-accuracy MAPE of <5% to support long-term operational planning. Additionally implemented a daily forecasting model using dynamic regression to capture non-linear weather and day-of-week patterns.

Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) R Programming Time Series Analysis

⚡ Solid State Transformer

Undergraduate capstone project: Designed and simulated a multi-stage, high-frequency Solid State Transformer (SST) in MATLAB/Simulink to provide active voltage regulation, reactive power compensation, and harmonic suppression for modern distribution grids. Implemented hysteresis band current control, sinusoidal PWM (SPWM), and an LCL filter, achieving a power factor of 0.99, harmonic distortion <5%, and efficiency >95%.

Control Systems Harmonic Analysis MATLAB Power Electronics Pulse-width Modulation (PWM) Simulink
