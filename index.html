👩‍💻 About

I’m a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Oklahoma, specializing in power systems, electrical machines, and energy technologies. My research focuses on the aging of electrical machines operating under dynamic operating conditions imposed by grid flexibility provision and how they impact the lifetime of electric motors. I explore control and data-driven techniques to solve pressing challenges in grid modernization.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I served for several years as an Electrical Engineer with Renewable Energy for Rural Livelihood (Nepal Government / UNDP Nepal), spearheading solar mini-grid projects, emergency technical rehabilitation, and formulating national standard feasibility guidelines.

Interests: Power systems modeling, electrical machine aging & thermal dynamics, grid flexibility, energy storage systems (BESS), power electronics, PLC & LabVIEW automation, signal processing, supervised machine learning.

📢 Opportunities: I am actively seeking research and engineering opportunities in Power Systems, Grid Modernization, Energy Storage, and Dynamic Machine Modeling.

🚀 Selected Projects

  • 💻 LabVIEW‑based Power Measurement System
    Developed a custom LabVIEW application to perform real-time acquisition, processing, and data logging of real, reactive, and apparent power for a Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) fed induction machine. Ensured measurement accuracy and reliability in strict compliance with relevant IEEE standards for experimental thermal and electrical analysis.
    Keywords: Data Acquisition (DAQ) · IEEE Standards · LabVIEW Programming · Power Measurement · VFDs
  • 🌡️ Temperature Control and Automation
    Designed a closed-loop automation system using a PLC and Ladder Logic for a temperature-controlled environment. Used cost-effective sensors and implemented safety features, real-time monitoring, and data logging using Click PLC and LED-based heater/fan indicators with potentiometer hardware simulation.
    Keywords: Control Systems · Data Logging · Lab Simulation · Ladder Logic · Click PLC
  • 🤖 Data Driven Temperature Estimation Techniques Using Supervised ML
    Investigated the feasibility of linear regression models (OLS, Ridge, Lasso) in Python (Scikit-learn) for real-time temperature estimation in a Permanent Magnet Synchronous Machine (PMSM) using a 300 MB dataset of operational parameters to prevent overheating. Demonstrated the necessity of non-linear and recurrent structures for motor thermal dynamics.
    Keywords: Data Preprocessing · Linear Regression · Python · Scikit-learn · Supervised ML
  • 🔋 Optimal Placement and Sizing of Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)
    Implemented a two-step approach using the Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) algorithm in Python (pandapower) for optimal placement and sizing of a Battery Energy Storage System in an electrical network. Demonstrated that optimal BESS placement on IEEE 14-bus and 30-bus test systems reduced power losses by 7.5%.
    Keywords: Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) · Power Flow Analysis · Python · pandapower
  • 📊 Analyzing and Forecasting Electricity Demand (ERCOT)
    Analyzed ERCOT electricity demand data (2016–2021) using time series decomposition to identify underlying trends, seasonality, and temperature correlations. Developed a seasonal ARIMA(0,1,2)(0,1,1)[12] forecasting model achieving <5% MAPE, alongside dynamic regression modeling.
    Keywords: Time Series Analysis · ARIMA · Dynamic Regression · R Programming
  • ⚡ Solid State Transformer (SST)
    Designed and simulated a multi-stage high-frequency Solid State Transformer in MATLAB/Simulink for modern distribution grids. Implemented hysteresis band current control, sinusoidal PWM (SPWM), and an LCL filter, achieving a power factor of 0.99, THD <5%, and efficiency >95%.
    Keywords: Control Systems · Harmonic Analysis · MATLAB/Simulink · Power Electronics · SPWM
