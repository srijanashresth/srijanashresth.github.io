Personal Profile

 I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Oklahoma. I earned my master's degree from the University of Oklahoma in 2024 and completed my undergraduate education at Tribhuvan University’s Institute of Engineering in Nepal.

Before starting my PhD, I worked for several years as an Electrical Engineer in my home country of Nepal. As an Electrical Engineer, I specialized in off-grid solar photovoltaic projects, including mini-grids for rural electrification and water pumping systems. My work has directly contributed to the development of national energy initiatives by providing technical expertise to policymakers.

Outside of my research, I enjoy badminton, table tennis, and cooking spicy food. I also like to relax with movies, books, music, and podcasts.
